Khmer Empire

The Khmer Empire was a powerful state in mainland Southeast Asia
that flourished from the 9th to the 15th centuries.
Centered in present-day Cambodia, it expanded across parts of
modern Thailand, Laos, and Vietnam, and left an enduring legacy
of monumental architecture, most notably the temples of Angkor.
